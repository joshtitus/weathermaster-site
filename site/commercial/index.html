Ten services. One crew.

Most projects need more than one kind of glazing. We handle all of it, so you're not coordinating three vendors on the same envelope.

We install curtain wall and facade systems on multi-story buildings across the metroplex. The sequence gets planned floor by floor so your envelope closes on time.

Our own glass robots lift panels weighing thousands of pounds. You don't wait on rental equipment or outside riggers to set the big ones.

We're equipped for high floors and complicated access. The crew plans each lift before the first crate ever reaches your site.

Storefront systems that look sharp on day one and hold up to daily traffic for years. We work in steel, wood, clad, and other materials.

Rated assemblies have to be installed exactly right to pass inspection. Because the same team handles your other glazing, rated sections tie in cleanly.

Custom doors in steel, wood, clad, and more, including imports from Germany, Italy, and Spain. We'll help you choose hardware that survives heavy use.

Ballistic glazing for facilities that need protection beyond the weather. These systems are heavy, and heavy is exactly what our equipment was built for.

Blast mitigation for doors and windows, installed by the same crew as the rest of your envelope. One accountable installer means fewer gaps.

Restoring an older building means matching profiles nobody stocks anymore. We replicate the look of the original while bringing it up to modern performance.

When stock parts won't fit the design, we turn to custom multi-dimensional casting. It's careful, detailed work that most glazing crews won't quote.

Questions we hear on every bid.

What does the free consultation include?

Joe Hiller comes to your site and walks the project with you. He'll look at the drawings, the access, the floor heights, and the panel sizes you're planning. You'll leave with a clear picture of what the job needs in equipment and sourcing. There's no charge and no obligation to hire us.

Do you work as a subcontractor for general contractors?

Yes, and it's a big part of what we do. We work with construction companies, architects, designers, and owners on projects of every size. We fit into your schedule and coordinate with the other trades on site. Tell us your timeline and we'll tell you honestly whether we can hit it.

How heavy a glass panel can you install?

Our in-house glass robots can lift panels weighing many thousands of pounds. That means jumbo glass installs don't depend on renting outside equipment. Every site is different, so access and floor height matter too. We'll confirm the right setup for your panels during the consultation.

Can you install imported or custom systems?

We supply and install mainstream and custom products in wood, steel, clad, vinyl, and other materials. We regularly buy from manufacturers in America, Germany, Italy, Spain, and other countries. If your architect specified something unusual, there's a good chance we've worked with it. If we haven't, we'll tell you up front.

Do you handle ballistic and blast glazing?

Yes. Ballistic windows and bomb blast door and window mitigation are both part of our commercial lineup. We also install fire-rated door and window wall systems. Keeping these with one installer means fewer handoffs and fewer gaps in the envelope.

Which areas do you serve?

We're based in Fort Worth and work across the Fort Worth and Dallas areas. Most of our commercial projects are within the metroplex. If your site is outside that range, call Joe anyway. He'll let you know whether we can take it on.
